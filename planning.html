Metric Website Marketplace Quick Commerce

Contribution margin = AOV − product cost (COGS%) − logistics − channel commission (where applicable). Quick Commerce bundles commission + fulfilment into one % with no separate logistics line, so it has no fixed "minimum profitable AOV" — margin holds as a constant % of AOV regardless of order size.
