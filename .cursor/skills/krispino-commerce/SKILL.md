# Krispino commerce, pricing, marketing

Apply these rules on every commercial change. The ₹1 Cr worksheet (`answers.html`) is the stretch plan. The trial tab (`trial.html`) is the realistic volume plan. Keep them separate.

## Ecommerce (India snacks / QC / D2C)

- Online geo is rest of India; community and QC start **Bengaluru then Hyderabad**. Not a Kerala market plan.
- QC is impulse, 5–15 SKUs per dark store, not the full catalogue. Zepto skews snacks/youth; Blinkit is broader metro + costly ads; Instamart is food-adjacent. **Prove Zepto 8 weeks before paying Blinkit.**
- Blinkit listing ~₹25k/SKU/state plus ~₹2–3 L/month min ads. Do not fund that from a ₹40 L first-window budget.
- QC customer MOV / free-delivery band is **₹199–₹299**. A ₹79–₹99 single is an *add-on*, not a basket. List a **₹199–₹249 combo** as the QC hero.
- QC MRP can sit **5–12% above website**. Commission ~25–35% bundled; treat launch ads ₹80–₹150/order as **acquisition**, not product profit.
- D2C site builds LTV (codes, WhatsApp, 120g reorder). QC buys the first yes.
- 70g trial must still be a real eat (not a token taste) so the customer reorders 120g.

## Product pricing

- Impulse trial band **₹50–₹150**. Krispino trials: ₹79–₹99 (60–70g). Everyday second purchase: **120g**, not 180g on first order.
- **Never ship one pouch on the site.** Per-order freight (₹70–₹120) kills sub-₹200 baskets. Default cart = 2 minis (₹199).
- **LOCKED: Krispino free shipping only when combined cart value ≥ ₹599.** Do not set ₹249, ₹349, or ₹699. Charge paid shipping below ₹599. Quick-commerce platform MOV (₹199–₹299) is their delivery rule, not ours.
- Check: paid ship below ₹599; free-ship combo / 120g mix must reach **₹599 combined**. `(₹599 − typical first cart) × add-on margin` should cover freight or keep collecting ₹59–₹90 ship.
- Thin/negative trial margin is allowed if booked as **CAC** and 30–60 day repeat to 120g is measured.
- Flipkart: keep every listing **under ₹1,000** (0% comm). Twins ₹249.
- Gross after film: small packs have **worse COGS %** than 180g. Do not assume 41% COGS on 70g; use ~44% until a BOM exists.
- Need ~55%+ gross to survive QC commission. If BOM cannot hit that, do not scale QC ads.

## Marketing strategy

- First-window goal must match traffic reality (~20 site sessions today). **₹40 L Sep–Dec / ~18k orders** is the working realistic number; ₹1 Cr stays on the other sheet.
- Mix for trial plan: **Website ~22 / MP ~15 / QC ~30 / WA ~28 / B2B ~5**. QC % is a Nov–Dec burst, not a Sep run-rate.
- Paid social sells the **₹199 twin PDP**. Influencers are **Bengaluru/Hyderabad nano, barter**, unique Shopflo codes. No ₹3k micros until 50 orders/code.
- Blended max CAC **₹75** on ₹199–₹219 AOV; **QC ads ₹80–₹150 at launch**, kill if no repeat. Pause 7-day CAC over ceiling for 2 weeks.
- Language: English 40%, Malayalam 25% (brand voice, not geo), Hindi 20%, Tamil 8%, Telugu 7%.
- Creative: crunch, 70g unbox, “two flavours / ₹199”. Themes: Dasara → Diwali → NY.
- If −15% vs target two months: cut paid social; pour into tastings, Flipkart twins, Zepto. Do not raise AOV back to ₹599 to “make rupees look bigger.”

## When editing numbers

- Recalc live tables from assumptions + channel %. Bump trial autosave key if defaults change.
- Never silently change `answers.html` commercial defaults.
- Show unit economics **by channel** (price − COGS − commission − freight − ads), not one blended margin.
